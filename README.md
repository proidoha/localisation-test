## Our Solar System

The Solar System consists of the **Sun** and all celestial objects gravitationally bound to it, including eight planets, their moons, asteroids, comets, and interplanetary dust.

### The Sun

Our nearest star is a **G-type main-sequence star** that has been burning for approximately 4.6 billion years.
